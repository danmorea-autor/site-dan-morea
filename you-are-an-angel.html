You Are an Angel

Some promises can only be kept when it's almost too late.

Patrick Adams receives the news that changes his life forever. With little time left, and a life spent always rushing past what mattered, he sets off on a journey through Rome, Paris, and London — the cities Lauren, the woman he loved, always dreamed of seeing. He doesn't just want to live her dream; he wants to finally give it to her, the way he never had the courage to do in time. A story about time, regret, and a love that remains, even after someone is gone.
