Follow Your Destiny

Sometimes, the road to someone runs through all the pain in the world.

A life that should have brought joy slowly turns into a destiny marked by suffering and disappointment. From betrayal and family heartbreak to the pain caused by others, David seems doomed to lose everything that matters — until he meets the beautiful Elena. Their happiness is soon overshadowed by her disappearance, and the real challenge of his life is only beginning. The road ahead is lined with obstacles, wrong choices, and a painful price to pay — but David refuses to give up on her.
