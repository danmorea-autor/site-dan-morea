About the author

Dan Morea writes about destiny, love, and the moments that change a person forever. His stories follow characters facing loss and regret, and the need to move forward — above all, the courage to begin again.

Every novel starts from the same conviction: that life deserves to be lived as a dream we choose, not a wait we endure.

— Dan Morea
